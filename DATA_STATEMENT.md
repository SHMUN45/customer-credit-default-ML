# Data Statement

- **Source:** Default of Credit Card Clients (Yeh, 2009), UCI, licence CC BY 4.0. One bank in Taiwan, 2005. Download steps in data/README.md.
- **Exclusions:** dropped ID and 35 duplicate rows; merged undocumented EDUCATION and MARRIAGE codes into "others"; kept outliers.
- **Limits:** one bank, one country, one year; small 51+ age group; PAY_ codes -2 and -1 undocumented.
- **Privacy:** anonymised, but contains sensitive attributes (SEX, AGE, MARRIAGE), so it's for research use only.
